FAQ

Before you register

Do I need a separate ticket for each Main Event flight?

No. Each flight is a separate buy-in, but you can play as many flights as you like. If you make Day 2 from more than one flight, only your largest stack carries forward and the smaller ones are refunded at the buy-in value.

What happens if an event does not reach its guarantee?

The guarantee stands. If entries fall short of the guaranteed prize pool, the difference is added as overlay and paid out in full according to the published payout structure.

Can I use a satellite ticket for a different event?

Tickets are valid for the event tier printed on them. A Main Event ticket cannot be split or exchanged for cash, but unused tickets can be applied to any equivalent-value event within the series before it ends.

Are the times on this page in my local timezone?

All times on this page are shown in UTC. The tournament lobby in the client displays start times converted to your device’s local timezone.

Is late registration available on every event?

Most events allow late registration for a set number of levels, typically between Level 12 and Level 18. The exact window is listed on each event’s detail panel in the lobby.
